## Cool yourself down today!

A smart table top fan, with adjustable speed and temperature sensing, all powered by usb c.

Perfect for any summer day, or any day... if you just want to move air around.

# Features

This "Cool" fan helps you always stay cool by having adjustable speed and the ability to turn on and off depending on the rooms temperature allowing you to stay cool without the extra work of turning a fan on and off, and on top of that the fan is from Noctua allowing it to be extremely silent as to not interfere with you.

# How to properly use it

The machine is used by plugging a USB C cable into the back, the device requires 5v at 1 Amp, and once its plugged it in does its startup and then you are good to go, turning the knob changes the speed and pressing the knob changes the modes allowing you to use the temperature mode and to turn that back off just turn the temperature requirement back down to zero. with device information available on the screen as well such as current room temp, target temprature and fan speed
